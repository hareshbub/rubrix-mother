The Problem We Solve

The Blackbox Effect

In most current systems, judging is a blackbox. Participants receive scores without transparent, actionable feedback on their architecture or code.

Zero Personal Connection

Judges evaluate a project in a vacuum. Current systems lack the context to analyze a builder's unique portfolio, background, and growth trajectory.

Judge Fatigue

Human evaluators get tired, and maintaining consistency across hundreds of teams is impossible. As events scale, evaluation quality plummets.
